Organisation 1

Organisation Name
Mutumane Kota and grilled chicken

Category
Small Business

What is the organisation about?
Mutumane Kota and grilled chicken is a local fast-food eatery specializing in traditional South
African street food and flame-grilled meats.

• What it does: Operates a daily takeaway and dine-in kitchen preparing freshly
cooked fast meals within the local community and convenient local delivery
services
• Products/Services offered: A variety of overloaded township-style kotas (filled with
chips, Russians , cheese, egg, and sauces), flame-grilled chicken meals, sides
(chakalaka, pap, fries), and soft drinks. They also provide local order pickup and
delivery
• Target audience: Students, local workforce, neighborhood families, and fast-food
enthusiasts looking for affordable, filling, and convenient meals.
Current Website

Does the organisation currently have a website? No

Motivation for Choosing this Organisation
• Lack of Digital Presence: The business currently operates without an official
website or online ordering platform uses WhatsApp for orders, relying entirely on
walk-in customers and  word-of-mouth.
• Business Growth Potential: Introducing a simple, mobile-friendly website with an
interactive menu, location finder, and online order pre-ordering system would
significantly reduce queue times during peak lunch hours and provide convenience
for the customers
• Streamlining Delivery & Ordering: Adding a modern, mobile-friendly website with
an online order-and-delivery form (including address selection and menu
customization) would drastically improve delivery efficiency, minimize order
errors, and boost sales volume.
• Brand Visibility: A dedicated online presence will help attract customers from
surrounding areas searching online for local food spots.

Organisation 2

Organisation Name
Hope Ahead Foundation (Anti-Drug Initiative)
Category
Non-Profit Organisation
 
What is the organisation about?
Hope Ahead Foundation is a community-driven non-profit organization dedicated to combating
substance abuse, supporting individuals through addiction recovery, and running preventative
educational programs.
 
• What it does: Hosts community awareness workshops, school outreach events,
support group meetings, and direct referrals to accredited rehabilitation centers.
• Products/Services offered: Substance abuse counseling, youth prevention
workshops, family support sessions, crisis helpline referrals, and educational
awareness pamphlets.
• Target audience: Youth at risk of substance abuse, individuals battling addiction,
families seeking support for loved ones, local schools, and community
donors/sponsors.

Current Website
Does the organisation currently have a website? No

Motivation for Choosing this Organisation
• Critical Need for Information Access: Individuals seeking help for addiction often
turn to online search engines first. A dedicated website would give vulnerable youth
and families instant access to support resources, emergency helpline contacts, and
meeting schedules in complete privacy.
• Donor and Volunteer Engagement: The organization relies heavily on community
donations and volunteer support. An online platform with direct donation links,
volunteer sign-up forms, and impact stories would greatly increase fundraising
capacity and community involvement.
• Public Awareness: A centralized website allows the NPO to regularly publish
educational blog posts, event dates, and success stories, enhancing its credibility
when applying for institutional funding or government grants
