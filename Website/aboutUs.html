ABOUT MUTUMANE KOTA AND GRILLED CHICKEN


Mutumane Kota & Grilled Chicken is a proud local fast-food spot dedicated to serving high-quality, authentic street food.

Known for our loaded township kotas and signature flame-grilled chicken, fish, and wings, we focus on bold flavor, fast turnaround, and reliable local delivery straight to your doorstep.
